HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY (COMPLIANT)

Clause 1.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] This policy governs all leave entitlements for permanent and contractual employees of the City Municipal Corporation (CMC).
Clause 1.2 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] This policy does not apply to daily wage workers or consultants. Those categories are governed by their respective contracts. 2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Clause 2.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Clause 2.2 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of joining.
Clause 2.3 | obligation: 14-day advance notice required (must)
Clause 2.4 | obligation: Written approval required before leave commences. Verbal not valid. (must)
Clause 2.5 | obligation: Unapproved absence = LOP regardless of subsequent approval (will)
Clause 2.6 | obligation: Max 5 days carry-forward. Above 5 forfeited on 31 Dec. (may / are forfeited)
Clause 2.7 | obligation: Carry-forward days must be used Jan–Mar or forfeited (must)
Clause 3.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Clause 3.2 | obligation: 3+ consecutive sick days requires medical cert within 48hrs (requires)
Clause 3.3 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.
Clause 3.4 | obligation: Sick leave before/after holiday requires cert regardless of duration (requires)
Clause 4.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births.
Clause 4.2 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks paid.
Clause 4.3 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.
Clause 4.4 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods. 5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
Clause 5.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.
Clause 5.2 | obligation: LWP requires Department Head AND HR Director approval (requires)
Clause 5.3 | obligation: LWP >30 days requires Municipal Commissioner approval (requires)
Clause 5.4 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Periods of LWP do not count toward service for the purposes of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits. 6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Clause 6.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as declared by the State Government each year.
Clause 6.2 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked.
Clause 6.3 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Compensatory off cannot be encashed. 7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
Clause 7.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.
Clause 7.2 | obligation: Leave encashment during service not permitted under any circumstances (not permitted)
Clause 7.3 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances. 8. GRIEVANCES
Clause 8.1 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department within 10 working days of the disputed decision.
Clause 8.2 | [VERBATIM SUMMARY CAUTION] Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
