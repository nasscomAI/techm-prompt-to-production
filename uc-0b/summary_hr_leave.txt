Summary of HR Leave Policy:

Clause 2.3: Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1.

Clause 2.4 [VERBATIM - RISK OF MEANING LOSS]: 2.4 Leave applications must receive written approval from the
    employee's direct manager before the leave commences.
    Verbal approval is not valid.

Clause 2.5 [VERBATIM - RISK OF MEANING LOSS]: 2.5 Unapproved absence will be recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP)
    regardless of subsequent approval.

Clause 2.6 [VERBATIM - RISK OF MEANING LOSS]: 2.6 Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave
    days to the following calendar year. Any days above 5 are
    forfeited on 31 December.

Clause 2.7 [VERBATIM - RISK OF MEANING LOSS]: 2.7 Carry-forward days must be used within the first quarter
    (January–March) of the following year or they are forfeited.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. ANNUAL LEAVE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Additional Clause 2.1: 2.1 Each permanent employee is entitled to 18 days of paid annual
    leave per calendar year.

Additional Clause 2.2: 2.2 Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month from the date of
    joining.

Additional Clause 3.1: 3.1 Each employee is entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per
    calendar year.

Additional Clause 3.3: 3.3 Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

Additional Clause 4.1: 4.1 Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity
    leave for the first two live births.

Additional Clause 4.2: 4.2 For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks
    paid.

Additional Clause 4.3: 4.3 Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave,
    to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

Additional Clause 4.4: 4.4 Paternity leave cannot be split across multiple periods.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Additional Clause 5.1: 5.1 An employee may apply for Leave Without Pay only after
    exhausting all applicable paid leave entitlements.

Additional Clause 5.4: 5.4 Periods of LWP do not count toward service for the purposes
    of seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Additional Clause 6.1: 6.1 Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays as
    declared by the State Government each year.

Additional Clause 6.2: 6.2 If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they
    are entitled to one compensatory off day, to be taken within
    60 days of the holiday worked.

Additional Clause 6.3: 6.3 Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Additional Clause 7.1: 7.1 Annual leave may be encashed only at the time of retirement
    or resignation, subject to a maximum of 60 days.

Additional Clause 7.3: 7.3 Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. GRIEVANCES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Additional Clause 8.1: 8.1 Leave-related grievances must be raised with the HR Department
    within 10 working days of the disputed decision.

Additional Clause 8.2: 8.2 Grievances raised after 10 working days will not be considered
    unless exceptional circumstances are demonstrated in writing.
