City Municipal Corporation - HR Leave Policy Summary

1. Purpose and Scope:
- Governs leave for permanent and contractual employees, excluding daily wage workers or consultants whose contracts dictate their terms (Clauses 1.1, 1.2).

2. Annual Leave:
- Permanent employees are entitled to 18 paid annual leave days per year, accruing at 1.5 days per month (Clauses 2.1, 2.2).
- Employees must submit a leave application at least 14 calendar days in advance using Form HR-L1 (Clause 2.3).
- Written approval from the direct manager must be received prior to leave commencement; verbal approval is not valid (Clause 2.4).
- Unapproved absence will result in Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of any subsequent approval (Clause 2.5).
- A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 5 is forfeited on 31 December (Clause 2.6).
- Carry-forward leave days must be utilized between January and March of the following year, or they are forfeited (Clause 2.7).

3. Sick Leave:
- Employees receive 12 paid sick leave days per year, which cannot be carried forward (Clauses 3.1, 3.3).
- Sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days strictly requires a medical certificate to be submitted within 48 hours of return (Clause 3.2).
- Taking sick leave immediately before or after a public holiday or annual leave requires a medical certificate, regardless of the leave duration (Clause 3.4).

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave:
- Female employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two live births, and 12 weeks for subsequent children (Clauses 4.1, 4.2).
- Male employees receive 5 days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 30 days of birth and cannot be split (Clauses 4.3, 4.4).

5. Leave Without Pay (LWP):
- Applicable only after exhausting all paid leaves. Time on LWP does not count towards service seniority or benefits (Clauses 5.1, 5.4).
- LWP strictly requires approval from BOTH the Department Head AND the HR Director; manager approval alone is insufficient (Clause 5.2).
- Any LWP period exceeding 30 continuous days requires approval from the Municipal Commissioner (Clause 5.3).

6. Public Holidays and Compensatory Off:
- Employees receive gazetted public holidays. Working on a holiday grants one compensatory off day valid for 60 days, which cannot be encashed (Clauses 6.1, 6.2, 6.3).

7. Leave Encashment:
- Annual leave can be encashed (max 60 days) only upon retirement or resignation (Clause 7.1).
- Leave encashment during active service is not permitted under any circumstances (Clause 7.2).
- Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed at all (Clause 7.3).

8. Grievances:
- Leave disputes must be raised with HR within 10 working days, unless written exceptional circumstances are proven (Clauses 8.1, 8.2).
